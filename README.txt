================================================================================
  HOOK & SWIM - HOW TO PLAY
================================================================================
 
  CONTROLS
  --------
  WASD        Move
  SHIFT       Sprint
  F           Interact (shops, fishing zones, chests)
  SPACE       Start game
  P           Pause
  ESC         Quit
  CTRL + T    Reset save
 
 
  GOAL
  ----
  Reach 1,000,000,000 coins to repay your debt and win.
  The first 700,000 are paid automatically while you play
  The last 300,000 must be paid in one shot at the DebtHolder
 
 
  EARN COINS
  ----------
  Human mode  ->  fish at the pier (press F on the fishing zone)
                  sell fish and chests to the Seller
  Fish mode   ->  collect glowing chests on the ocean floor
 
 
  SPEND COINS
  -----------
  IncomeMultiplier  ->  earn more coins per sale
  StaminaShop       ->  sprint longer
  DebtHolder        ->  pay off your debt
 
 
  WATCH OUT
  ---------
  - Enemies in fish mode steal your coins on contact
  - If coins drop below 0, you lose all chests in your inventory
